# Flashback and Swedish study forums

Flashback's rules prohibit advertising, and its readers detect it faster
than most. The only workable posture is a person answering a question in
their own name, with the interest disclosed in the same post.

**Where.** Flashback → Utbildning → Universitet och högskola. Threads about
MBA, Handelshögskolan, and studying abroad. Search first: a GMAT thread
almost certainly already exists, and reviving a dead one to plug something
is transparent.

**Never.** A new thread announcing the product. A second account agreeing
with the first. A "someone recommended this to me" framing.

## Other Swedish forums

**Studentum, Allastudier and similar portals.** These sell listings.
That is honest advertising and needs no disguise — but read what a listing
actually costs and what traffic it delivers before paying, and treat the
number they quote as a sales figure rather than a measurement.

**Facebook groups for Swedish business students.** Covered in
`facebook-discord.md`; the rule is the same, and most of them require
admin permission for anything promotional. Ask first. An admin who says
yes is worth more than a post that gets removed.

**University Discords and Slacks.** Almost always run by a student union
with a no-advertising rule. Ask a moderator. If they say no, that is the
end of it — posting anyway costs the whole channel permanently.

## The thing that makes all of this work or not

Every one of these communities has been marketed at by test-prep companies
for years, and they are good at spotting it. The only durable advantage is
that the disclosure is true and the advice stands without the link. Both
drafts above are written so that if the last paragraph were deleted, the
post would still be worth reading. That is the test.
