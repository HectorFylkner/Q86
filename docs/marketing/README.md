# Marketing kit

Everything here is a draft to be read, edited and *then* posted by a
person. **Nothing in this directory has been posted anywhere**, and no
account has been created on any platform on Q86's behalf.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|

| [messaging.md](messaging.md) | The claims, the words, and what may never be said |
| [launch-checklist.md](launch-checklist.md) | What has to be true before anything is posted, in order |
| [channels/](channels) | One file per channel, with drafts ready to edit |

## The rules these drafts follow

Four, and they are not stylistic preferences. Two are law, one is
platform policy, and one is the reason the product exists.

**1. Every draft says who wrote it.** No post pretends to be a
disinterested recommendation. On Reddit, in a Facebook group, in a forum
thread, the first or second sentence says that this is the person who
built it. A recommendation that hides its author is astroturfing, it is
against every one of these platforms' rules, and under
marknadsföringslagen § 9 it is an unlawful failure to identify marketing
as marketing.

**2. Every draft is useful even if nobody clicks.** The guide, the number,
the correction — that has to stand on its own. A post whose only content
is a link is spam whatever it says about itself.

**3. No claim without a basis.** No score guarantee. No invented user
counts, testimonials, reviews or "students say". No comparative claim
about a competitor that has not been checked against their current
material. Marknadsföringslagen § 10 makes an unsubstantiated claim
unlawful, and the burden of proof sits with the advertiser.

**4. GMAT™ and GMAT Focus Edition™ are GMAC trademarks.** Q86 is
independent and unaffiliated. Never imply endorsement, never use GMAC's
logo, never describe questions as official.

## What is deliberately absent

- **Testimonials.** The platform is new and has no users to quote.
  Inventing them would be trivial and unlawful.
- **User counts and "join N students".** Same reason.
- **Score-improvement claims** ("+6 points in 4 weeks"). There is no data,
  and there could not honestly be until there are cohorts.
- **Comparison tables against named competitors.** Possible later, with
  their current material open in another window and a date on the table.
- **Paid advertising copy.** Nothing here is written for an ad platform,
  because the first thing to find out is whether the free channels work.

## How to use a draft

1. Read it as the person who will be answering the replies.
2. Cut anything that has become untrue since it was written.
3. Fill in every `[bracket]`. They are facts only the owner has.
4. Post it yourself, from your own account, and stay to answer.
